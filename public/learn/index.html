Personal build · Local AI prototype

Meet Moxie, the transplant sidekick.

I’m a liver transplant patient. I built Moxie to organize information and help me prepare questions for my transplant team. The public examples use fictional details.

Moxie runs on a local model, has no MyChart connection, and is an educational prototype—not a clinician or a replacement for the care team.

What Moxie does

Sort the details. Prep the questions.

Moxie can organize supplied lab dates into a timeline and suggest questions. A small local keyword search can provide general patient-education notes with their source titles.

Safety by design

The care team stays in charge.

  • No diagnosis or lab interpretation.
  • No medication-change advice.
  • Missed-dose questions take a fixed route to the patient’s own team, without a model call.

The public Learn page does not connect to Moxie. Its local private-profile mode is separate; no personal care-plan note appears here.

The Valley Tote case study

The website was the easy part.
Getting a customer was harder.

We used AI to turn a moving-tote business concept into a website. The project developed into a public site with a mascot, inquiry forms and payment infrastructure. The initial build was fast. The early test gave us a separate question to answer: would interested visitors take the next step?
